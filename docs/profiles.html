Q is measured relative to the mass-weighted center of the entire roll. Its average weighted by R is therefore zero, even when individual layers are displaced.

Density — the sum

R(ζ) = d + b1(ζ) + b2(ζ)ℓ

R is invariant under θ₁ ↔ θ₂. For θ₁ = θ − ε and θ₂ = θ + ε, ∂R/∂ε at ε = 0 is zero: small splitting enters at second order.

Center of mass — the difference

Q(ζ) = b2 − b12 − C

Q reverses sign when the angles are exchanged and responds linearly to small asymmetry. Joint fitting of R and Q distinguishes the left and right branches.

Full model and fitting objective

h = 1/2 − δ; bᵢ is the circular meniscus bulge relative to its wall-contact point. For |ζ| < h:

bᵢ(ζ) = − cos θᵢ (h² − ζ²)√(h² − cos²θᵢ ζ²) + h sin θᵢ

This expression is regular at 90°. Uᵢ = ∫ bᵢ dζ and Vᵢ = ∫ bᵢ² dζ over [−h, h]; A = 2hd + U₁ + U₂.

C = d(U₂ − U₁) + V₂ − V₁2A,   d = φℓ − 2U(θ₀, h)2h

With volume conservation, U₁ + U₂ = 2U(θ₀, h). When angles vary independently, the area deviation from φℓ is displayed. For freely chosen angles, rec/adv labels identify interface positions, even if the angle ordering is reversed.

χ² = ∑k ∈ Iρ (Rk − R̄k)²s²R,k + ∑k ∈ Iq (Qk − Q̄k)²s²Q,k

For MD fitting, predictions are integrated over the actual finite bins; empty q bins are skipped and near-wall layers are excluded. This explorer shows continuous analytical profiles of uniform liquid; molecular layering and vapor are not modeled.
