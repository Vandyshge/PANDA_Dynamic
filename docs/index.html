Force balance

2Lyγlv(cos θrec − cos θadv) = Feff

The factor of 2 accounts for both pore walls. Additional resistance is subtracted from the total external force.

Volume conservation

G(θrec) + G(θadv) = 2G(θ₀)
G(θ) = 2θ + sin 2θ − π8 cos²θ

Angles in the equations are in radians. At θ = π/2, the continuous limit is G = 0; G′(π/2) = 1/6.

Linear approximation and model limits

Write θrec = θ₀ + u and θadv = θ₀ + v. Then G′(θ₀)(u + v) = 0 and 2Lyγlvsin θ₀(v − u) = Feff.

θrec,adv ≈ θ₀ ∓ Feff4Lyγlvsin θ₀

The mean angle changes only at second order. The model assumes circular menisci, constant volume, and fixed wall-contact separation. If no angle pair in [0°, 180°] exists at a given force, the curve stops at its geometric limit.

For vx = bFext, angle data determine γth = γlv/(1 − βb). They cannot determine γlv and β independently. Constant resistance illustrates a threshold closure; it does not establish a dry-friction law.

Analytical predictions from the PANDA-D manuscript, not new MD measurements. The defaults γlv = 0.398 and Ly = 18.624 are parameters from the original study.
